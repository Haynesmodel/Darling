Reassigned all regular-season week labels in these two seasons using seven-day intervals from their opening Sundays (September 11, 2016 and September 8, 2019). The previous labels treated stray dates as separate weeks and then capped later weeks at 13. Each corrected season has 13 weeks with five games and exactly one appearance per team per week. Scores and season totals are unchanged.

The leaders after six completed weeks remain Zook (5–1) in 2016 and Zubs (6–0) in 2019. The October 30, 2016 date is a schedule reconstruction rather than an independently verified platform export.
